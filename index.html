Caring from a distance is still caring. It just needs someone trustworthy on the ground.

Why OBIK began

Our work stays focused on women 65 and older — not as a slogan, but as a design choice for how we allocate support.

Why we exist

Too many older women are navigating systems alone.

Financial pressure, fragmented healthcare, and everyday tasks that grow harder with age — often without a steady person to call.

When children and relatives live in another city or country, coordination gets harder. Services may exist, but finding them, affording them, and trusting them is another story.

OBIK is the link between intention and action: families who want to help, institutions that can deliver, and women who deserve both.

What we do

Three ways we show up.

Each programme solves a different gap. Most women we serve touch more than one.

1. OBIK Diaspora Care

1. OBIK Diaspora Care

Bringing trusted support closer, even when families are far away.

Distance should not prevent families from ensuring their loved ones receive consistent practical support.

OBIK helps diaspora and geographically distant families coordinate everyday assistance for older loved ones back home.

Our approach focuses on practical care coordination, household support, wellbeing check-ins, healthcare arrangements and connections to trusted local service providers.

02. Upkeep & Protection

02. OBIK Upkeep & Social Protection

Strengthening financial security and access to essential support.

Financial insecurity can significantly affect a woman's ability to live independently as she grows older.

OBIK works to improve access to social protection programmes and mobilize resources that provide additional financial assistance to older women experiencing vulnerability.

Through benefit-access assistance, sponsored upkeep allocations and community partnerships, we aim to help women access the resources they need for everyday living.

03. Health & Mobility

03. OBIK Health Access & Mobility

Making healthcare easier to access and navigate.

Access to healthcare involves more than the availability of medical facilities. Affordability, transportation, referrals and practical assistance can all influence whether an older woman receives the care she needs.

OBIK works with healthcare institutions, licensed insurance providers and relevant service partners to improve and strengthen healthcare access through institutional partnerships, care coordination and medical mobility, coordinating medical support and facilitating transport for medical appointments.

Our approach brings healthcare access and practical mobility together to make navigating healthcare easier for older women.

Together, these programmes address different but interconnected aspects of women's ageing and wellbeing.

Our commitment

Dignity, accountability, and respect at every stage of later life.

Older women should never be treated as an afterthought. Every programme, check-in, and partnership at OBIK is built on clear standards of care and transparency.

Person-first care

Support is shaped around each woman's voice, independence, and lived reality — never one-size-fits-all charity.

Verified local trust

Every provider, coordinator, and institutional partner is vetted so families near and far have genuine peace of mind.

Transparent stewardship

Resources, upkeep allocations, and sponsored medical support are tracked with clear reporting to families and funders.

Long-term continuity

We commit to steady, ongoing relationships rather than one-off interventions that leave women unsupported later.

OBIK (“we”, “our”, or “us”) is committed to protecting the privacy, dignity, and personal data of the older women we serve, their families, partners, and visitors to our website.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect information you voluntarily provide when requesting care support, inquiring about diaspora coordination, applying to partner, or making a donation—such as your name, contact details, and relevant care preferences.
