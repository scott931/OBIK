ABOUT OBIK • WHO WE ARE

Improving the experience of ageing for women.

OBIK — Strengthening the support systems women need to age with dignity.

OBIK is an organization working to improve the wellbeing, independence and quality of life of women as they age. Through care coordination, financial support programmes, healthcare partnerships and community-based support, we connect women and their families to the resources and services they need to live with dignity.

We recognize that ageing brings changing needs, and that access to financial resources, healthcare and reliable practical support can make a significant difference in a woman's quality of life.

Yet these resources are not equally accessible.

Across African communities, many older women navigate financial insecurity, healthcare barriers and limited support networks. Families may be separated by migration and work, while some women have little or no dependable family support.

OBIK works to address these gaps through interconnected programmes, institutional partnerships and community-based solutions.

Our work is centered on strengthening access, improving coordination and building support systems that enable women to maintain their dignity and independence as they age.

A Note From the Founder

OBIK Origins
“Caring about an older loved one and being able to provide consistent support are not always the same thing.”

OBIK began with a simple observation: caring about an older loved one and being able to provide consistent support are not always the same thing.

Family members may live in different countries or cities, and everyday needs can become difficult to coordinate across distance. Through personal experiences and a growing understanding of the challenges facing older women, I began to see that this was part of a much wider issue.

For some women, the challenge is coordinating support with family members who live elsewhere. For others, it is access to food, financial resources, healthcare or simply having someone who can help them navigate the services available to them.

I began to see an opportunity to approach these challenges differently: by bringing together practical care coordination, financial support, healthcare access and stronger community connections.

That thinking became OBIK.

Today, our ambition is to contribute to a future in which African women can grow older with greater independence, dignity and access to the support they need.

We believe ageing deserves greater attention, better solutions and stronger partnerships.

Dalcy C.

Founder, OBIK
