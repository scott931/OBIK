Partner with OBIK

Help us build a better experience of aging.

Improving the experience of aging requires more than good intentions. It requires resources, collaboration and support systems that can respond to the realities older women face.

OBIK welcomes partnerships with foundations, development organizations, healthcare institutions, insurers, corporate partners, community organizations, philanthropists and individuals who share our commitment to dignity, independence and improved wellbeing in later life.

You can work with us by:

  • Funding the upkeep of financially vulnerable older women.
  • Supporting healthcare access and medical mobility initiatives.
  • Partnering in community-based outreach and support.
  • Providing professional expertise, healthcare services, and institutional collaboration.
  • Supporting the development, implementation, and expansion of OBIK's programs.

Together, we can help ensure that growing older does not mean growing without support.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

OBIK (“we”, “our”, or “us”) is committed to protecting the privacy, dignity, and personal data of the older women we serve, their families, partners, and visitors to our website.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect information you voluntarily provide when requesting care support, inquiring about diaspora coordination, applying to partner, or making a donation—such as your name, contact details, and relevant care preferences.

2. How We Use Information

Personal information is used strictly to coordinate practical support for women aged 65+, respond to family or partner inquiries, process programme sponsorships, and maintain transparent accountability. We never sell or rent personal data to third parties.

3. Data Protection & Your Rights

We apply strict confidentiality safeguards across all local coordinators and healthcare partners. You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal records at any time by contacting our team.
