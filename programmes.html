Our Programmes

Building better support systems for older women.

Aging brings different needs at different stages of life. For some women, the challenge is financial insecurity. For others, it is accessing healthcare, maintaining independence, or having someone dependable nearby. For families living far away, ensuring an older loved one receives consistent support can be equally challenging.

OBIK addresses these interconnected challenges through three complementary programs: Diaspora Care, Upkeep & Social Protection, and Health Access & Mobility.

Each program addresses a different aspect of aging while working toward a shared goal: helping older women live with dignity, independence, security, and access to the support they need.

OBIK Diaspora Care

Keeping families connected. Bringing everyday support closer.

Distance should never mean disconnection from the people who matter most.

Across Kenya and beyond, many families live far from their older loved ones. Children and relatives may live in different cities, counties, or countries, often relying on phone calls, occasional visits, and financial support to stay involved in their well-being.

Yet staying connected is not always the same as knowing whether someone is doing well, whether they need assistance, or whether the support they require is reaching them.

An older woman may need help arranging a medical appointment, accessing essential services, attending to everyday needs, or simply having someone dependable to turn to. For families living at a distance, coordinating this support consistently can be difficult.

OBIK Diaspora Care is designed to bridge this gap through a connected support system that brings older adults, their families, and trusted local assistance closer together.

Our approach

At the center of this program is the OBIK Connect experience: a simple, accessible, senior-friendly digital platform designed to make communication, family involvement, and everyday support easier for older adults.

Through a dedicated tablet provided as part of the Diaspora Care offering, older adults will have a more accessible way to connect with family, receive messages, share moments, and access available support.

Designed with simplicity and language accessibility in mind, the experience will progressively incorporate Kenyan languages and voice-enabled features, helping older adults interact with technology in ways that feel familiar and natural.

The tablet will be part of a broader ecosystem connecting three key elements: the older adult, their family, and OBIK's local support network.

Through this program, OBIK aims to facilitate:

  • Family connection

    Simple video calls, voice messages, photographs, and shared moments that help older adults maintain meaningful relationships with family members, regardless of distance.

  • Accessible digital interaction

    A simplified, senior-friendly tablet experience designed around ease of use, with progressive support for Kenyan languages and voice interaction.

  • Family involvement and updates

    A companion digital experience through which authorized family members can stay connected, communicate, and coordinate agreed aspects of their loved one's support.

  • Wellbeing and everyday support

    Agreed check-ins and a pathway for older adults or their families to request assistance when everyday needs arise.

  • Healthcare coordination

    Support with arranging appointments, medication collection, referrals and other healthcare-related needs where appropriate.

  • Local assistance and service coordination

    Connecting families and older adults to appropriate local support, including household assistance, transport and other practical services.

The digital platform is intended to make communication and coordination easier, while OBIK's local support network helps connect those interactions to practical assistance on the ground.

What this looks like in practice

Imagine a daughter living in Seattle whose mother lives in rural Kenya.

Through OBIK Diaspora Care, her mother receives an OBIK Connect tablet configured to provide a simple way to communicate with her family and access agreed support services.

She can make video calls, receive photographs and voice messages, and use the device to reach the appropriate support channels. As the platform develops, she will also be able to interact with voice-enabled features in her preferred language, making the experience more accessible without having to navigate complicated technology.

From Seattle, her daughter can connect through the companion family platform, share messages, arrange calls, and remain involved in agreed aspects of her mother's support.

If her mother needs help arranging a medical appointment, coordinating transport, or attending to an everyday need, the family can engage OBIK to coordinate appropriate local support.

The family remains connected, the older woman retains an active role in decisions about her own life, and OBIK provides the coordination that helps bridge the distance.

The value of this program goes beyond providing a communication device. It creates a dependable connection between families, older adults, and the practical support available within their communities.

Who benefits?

  • Older adults whose children or relatives live in different cities, counties, or countries.
  • Families in the diaspora seeking a more consistent, dependable way to stay involved in the wellbeing of loved ones back home.
  • Older adults who would benefit from accessible digital communication and easier connections to local support.
  • Families who need assistance coordinating everyday services, healthcare arrangements and practical support for older relatives.

Partnership opportunities

OBIK welcomes collaboration with technology manufacturers, software developers, telecommunications providers, diaspora associations, community organizations, healthcare institutions and local service providers.

Partnership opportunities include:

  • Technology and device partnerships

    Supporting the development, sourcing, and deployment of senior-friendly OBIK Connect tablets.

  • Digital infrastructure and innovation

    Collaborating on the development of OBIK's multilingual platform, voice-enabled interaction and accessible digital experiences.

  • Connectivity partnerships

    Exploring affordable internet and mobile connectivity arrangements for older adults, particularly in underserved communities.

  • Community and service partnerships

    Building trusted local networks that can respond to practical support needs.

  • Healthcare partnerships

    Strengthening connections to healthcare providers, appointment coordination, and medical mobility services.

Through these collaborations, OBIK aims to develop a connected support model that can be adapted to the realities of aging, family life and digital access in Kenya and, over time, across the wider African region.

OBIK Upkeep & Social Protection

Strengthening financial security and independence in later life.

Financial insecurity can make growing older particularly difficult. Many older women have little or no reliable income, leaving them dependent on inconsistent family support or struggling to meet their everyday needs.

While government social protection programs provide important assistance to eligible older persons, some women remain unreached, struggle to navigate the application process, or find available assistance insufficient to meet their basic needs.

OBIK seeks to address these gaps through a combination of social protection navigation and sponsored financial upkeep.

Our approach

The program has two complementary areas of intervention:

A

Social protection and benefit-access assistance

OBIK helps older women understand and navigate existing social protection systems, particularly where they may be eligible for government assistance but face barriers accessing it.

This may include:

  • Identifying women who may qualify for available social protection benefits.
  • Guiding eligibility requirements and application procedures.
  • Assisting with documentation and connecting women with the relevant government offices.
  • Supporting application follow-ups through appropriate official channels.
  • Working with community organizations and relevant public institutions to improve awareness of available benefits.

OBIK does not replace government social protection programs or determine eligibility. Our role is to help women navigate the systems and access assistance for which they may qualify.

B

Sponsored upkeep allocations

For older women who lack reliable income or adequate financial support, OBIK is developing a sponsored upkeep program through which donors, foundations, corporate partners and institutional supporters can contribute towards regular financial allocations.

OBIK will identify and assess eligible women based on their circumstances and support needs, and administer agreed allocations to help meet essential living expenses.

The intention is to provide predictable financial assistance that gives women greater freedom to manage their everyday needs, make personal decisions and maintain a degree of financial independence.

What this looks like in practice

Consider an older woman living in a rural community who has no dependable source of income and struggles to meet her basic needs.

Through the OBIK Upkeep Fund, she may be assessed and enrolled for sponsored monthly financial assistance, subject to program eligibility and available funding.

A donor or institutional partner can support the upkeep of one woman or contribute towards a group of beneficiaries. OBIK administers the agreed support and maintains appropriate records of allocations and program progress.

Alongside this, OBIK can help women who may qualify for existing government benefits navigate the relevant application processes.

Who benefits?

  • Older women with little or no reliable income.
  • Women experiencing financial insecurity or inadequate family support.
  • Older women who may qualify for government social protection but face barriers accessing it.
  • Women whose existing sources of support are insufficient to meet their basic needs.

Partnership and funding opportunities

OBIK invites foundations, philanthropic organizations, corporate partners, diaspora communities, philanthropists, and individual donors to support the upkeep program.

Contributions can help finance regular beneficiary allocations, strengthen social protection navigation, and support outreach to financially vulnerable older women.

OBIK Health Access & Mobility

Making healthcare more accessible, affordable and within reach.

Having a healthcare facility nearby does not necessarily mean an older woman can access the care she needs.

The cost of consultations, medication, diagnostics and transport can create significant barriers. For women living in rural or underserved communities, distance, mobility limitations and a lack of practical assistance can make accessing healthcare even more difficult.

OBIK seeks to improve access to appropriate healthcare by building partnerships with healthcare institutions, licensed insurers and relevant service providers, while addressing the practical challenges that prevent older women from receiving care.

Our approach

The program focuses on three complementary areas:

A

Affordable healthcare access

OBIK seeks to establish partnerships with licensed insurance providers, hospitals, clinics and other healthcare institutions to explore affordable group-based healthcare arrangements and negotiated access to services.

Rather than expecting every older woman to navigate and finance healthcare independently, OBIK aims to work with institutions to explore arrangements that make appropriate healthcare more accessible to enrolled beneficiaries.

Potential areas of collaboration include:

  • Affordable group medical cover arrangements.
  • Access to outpatient and specialist consultations.
  • Diagnostic services and referrals.
  • Medication access and pharmacy partnerships.
  • Preventive health education and awareness.

Specific benefits and coverage will depend on the agreements reached with licensed insurers and healthcare providers.

B

Medical transport and mobility

For some older women, getting to a healthcare facility is as challenging as paying for the treatment itself.

OBIK aims to coordinate practical medical mobility arrangements for women who experience difficulties accessing healthcare because of transport costs, distance, or mobility challenges.

This may include:

  • Coordinating transport for scheduled medical appointments.
  • Exploring designated medical transport days for communities.
  • Facilitating appointment scheduling and referrals.
  • Coordinating accompaniment where required and available.
  • Supporting return journeys following medical visits.

C

Health education and awareness

Through partnerships with qualified healthcare professionals and institutions, OBIK aims to facilitate health education and awareness initiatives that help older women make informed decisions about their wellbeing.

These may include preventive health education, women's health awareness, appropriate referrals, and access to relevant healthcare information.

What this looks like in practice

Imagine several older women in a rural community have scheduled hospital appointments in Nairobi but struggle to arrange transport.

Through appropriate healthcare and transport partnerships, OBIK could coordinate designated medical transport days, helping organize collection, appointment schedules, accompaniment where available, and return journeys.

At the institutional level, OBIK could also work with a licensed insurer or healthcare network to explore affordable group healthcare arrangements for enrolled women.

The goal is to make healthcare access more practical and coordinated, rather than leaving older women to navigate every barrier alone.

Who benefits?

  • Older women with limited access to affordable healthcare.
  • Women who face financial or practical barriers to medical appointments.
  • Older women in rural and underserved communities.
  • Women who require assistance navigating healthcare services and referrals.

Partnership opportunities

OBIK welcomes partnerships with licensed insurers, hospitals, clinics, diagnostic centers, pharmacies, transport providers, healthcare professionals and women's health organizations.

These collaborations can help develop affordable healthcare arrangements, improve medical mobility and extend access to appropriate health services.

Who benefits from our programmes?

Supporting older women. Strengthening the systems around them.

OBIK prioritizes older women, particularly those experiencing financial insecurity, limited access to healthcare, or inadequate family and community support.

We recognize that the experience of aging is not the same for every woman. Some need financial assistance, others need help accessing healthcare, and many face several challenges at once.

Our programs are designed to respond to these different circumstances while strengthening the support systems around older women.

Our priority includes:

  • Older women with little or no reliable income.
  • Women who lack consistent family or community support.
  • Older women facing barriers to healthcare and medical transport.
  • Women who may be eligible for existing social protection but struggle to access it.
  • Older adults whose families live elsewhere and need dependable local support.

We aim to reach women whose circumstances make them particularly vulnerable to financial insecurity, isolation and limited access to essential services, while respecting their independence, choices and individual needs.
